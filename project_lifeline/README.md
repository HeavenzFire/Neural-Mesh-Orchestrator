# 🎗️ Project LIFELINE: Zero-Balance Childhood Cancer Initiative

> **"No child should fight for their life while their parents fight a bank."**

Project LIFELINE is the first operational deployment of the **Syntropic Surplus Engine**, dedicated exclusively to eliminating the financial burden of pediatric cancer in the United States.

## 🚀 Mission

**Goal:** Identify every child in America currently battling cancer and pay 100% of their medical bills, travel costs, and family support needs—immediately, automatically, and permanently.

**Target:** ~15,000 new diagnoses/year + ~40,000 active treatments = **$4.2 Billion annual coverage**.

## 🏗️ Architecture

### 1. Surplus Minter (`core/surplus_minter.py`)
Generates `LIFE` tokens based on verified regenerative actions:
- Grid stability improvements
- Water purification metrics
- Data healing operations
- Infrastructure resilience scores

*Unlike crypto mining, this creates value from real-world positive utility.*

### 2. Patient Registry Oracle (`core/patient_oracle.py`)
Securely interfaces with:
- Children's Oncology Group (COG) hospitals
- Insurance claim databases (HIPAA-compliant)
- Ronald McDonald House networks
- State Medicaid programs

*Uses Zero-Knowledge Proofs to verify diagnosis without exposing patient identity.*

### 3. Auto-Pay Engine (`core/auto_pay_engine.py`)
Instantly disburses funds to:
- **Hospitals:** Direct payment of treatment invoices
- **Families:** Stipends for travel, lodging, lost wages
- **Research:** Funding for rare cancer trials
- **Support:** Mental health, sibling care, grief counseling

### 4. Transparency Ledger (`data/ledger.json`)
Public, immutable record of:
- Tokens minted (source verification)
- Bills paid (anonymized hashes)
- Families supported (count only)
- Remaining surplus

## 💰 Economic Model

## 🛡️ Compliance & Security

- **HIPAA Compliant:** Patient data never leaves hospital firewalls; only ZK-proofs of diagnosis are processed.
- **SOC 2 Type II:** Infrastructure audited for data security.
- **IRS 501(c)(3) Bridge:** Legal wrapper for tax-deductible traditional donations alongside crypto surplus.
- **State Medical Boards:** Direct integration for bill verification.
